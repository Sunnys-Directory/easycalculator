EMI Calculator (Simple Interest)

Calculate loan payments using Flat Rate Interest.

How it works

This calculator uses the Simple Interest formula: (Principal × Rate × Months) / 100.

This is commonly used for flat-rate loans or personal lending where the interest is calculated on the full principal amount for the entire duration.
