What Does Your BMI Mean?

In the US, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy. Values above or below may indicate health risks.

Is BMI Accurate?

BMI is a screening tool and does not measure body fat directly. Athletes and muscular individuals may have higher BMI.
